## What we use and from where

- Source: `mods-src/clouds/` — **Real Skybox** by **Junior_Djjr** (MixMods.com.br), v0.92.
- We use ONLY the textures inside `realskybox/skyboxes.txd` (15 cloud panoramas, 2048², + a stars texture
  we do not need — our stars are procedural). The ASI code, the `skybox.dff` dome mesh and the
  `skyboxes.dat` config are NOT used: the engine samples its own analytic dome in `fsSky`, and the
  weather→texture mapping is re-authored in our converter.

## Why permission is non-trivial

The readme documents MIXED authorship inside the TXD: some textures are **Ezekiel's** sky textures, some
were generated by Junior_Djjr in **Terragen 4** (Terragen's own output-licensing terms apply to the free
tier). A blanket OK from Junior may not cover Ezekiel's work — the permission must name the specific
textures we ship.
